How it works

Three steps, one of them human.

Takeoff is done on every project, takes days by hand, and mistakes in it are expensive. QtoMate takes over the measuring and leaves the judgement to the estimator.

01

Upload the drawing set

Add the PDF sheets for the package you are pricing: plans, sections, schedules.

02

QtoMate measures and counts

It reads scales and dimensions, measures lengths and areas, counts elements, and drafts the quantity lines.

03

The estimator reviews

Each line points back to the sheet it came from. You check, correct, and approve before it becomes a bill of quantities.

What it does

Built around how estimators actually work.

The goal is not to replace the quantity surveyor. It is to remove the hours spent clicking around drawings.

Takeoff from PDF drawings

Lengths, areas and counts measured directly from the sheets you already receive from the designer.

Traceable quantities

Every quantity keeps a reference to its drawing and location, so a reviewer can see where a number came from.

More than one standard

Developed on NRM2 and US imperial takeoff conventions, with regional norm bases planned next.

Your rates, not ours

QtoMate produces quantities and a draft estimate from the rates you provide. It does not invent prices.

Who it is for

Teams that price from drawings.

  • Contractors and subcontractors preparing bids
  • Quantity surveyors and estimating departments
  • Cost and planning engineers checking designer quantities
  • Freelance estimators handling several projects at once

About

Made by a construction professional.

QtoMate is being built by Begench Begmuratov, who has more than ten years of experience on construction projects with international contractors and now works in planning, cost estimating and quantity takeoff.

The product is built on Claude, Anthropic's AI model, combined with purpose-built tools for measuring PDF drawings.

Where we are: QtoMate is in early development. A working prototype exists and has been tested on a real project drawing set. We are looking for a small number of pilot users to shape the first release.
