Count takeoff

Counting items priced per piece: doors, windows, sockets, detectors, fittings.

Each item gets a mark on the sheet, so you can see at a glance which ones are already counted. One line per type keeps the bill readable: “Door 900 mm”, “Smoke detector”, not “Items”.

Legends and schedules on the sheet tell you what each symbol means. Count from the plan and compare with the schedule; a difference is worth a question to the designer.

Worked example

Three doors on the plan against a door schedule listing four: one is missing from the plan, or the schedule is out of date.

In QtoMate

The Count tool (C) places a numbered mark with each click. Press Enter to finish a type, then start the next one as a new line.

Questions

Can I fix a misplaced mark?

Yes. Select the line, then drag the mark, or double-click it to remove it. Undo works too.

Does the count snap to symbols?

No. Count marks go exactly where you click, which is all a count needs.

Related terms

  • Bill of quantitiesThe list of every item of work and material in a project, each with a unit and a quantity.
