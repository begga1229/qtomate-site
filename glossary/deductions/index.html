Deductions for openings

Subtracting the parts of a surface that will not be built or finished, such as windows in a wall or a shaft in a slab.

Measuring the gross area and deducting openings separately keeps both numbers visible. If an opening changes, only its line changes.

Measurement rules often say small openings are not deducted, for example those under a set size. Check the rules for your contract before deducting everything.

Worked example

A wall 12.00 m long and 3.00 m high is 36.00 m². Two windows of 1.60 × 1.50 m are deducted: 36.00 − 2 × 2.40 = 31.20 m² of plaster.

In QtoMate

Draw the opening as an area and tick “Deduct”. It is hatched on the sheet, shows as negative in the bill and is subtracted from its group’s subtotal.

Questions

Do I deduct the opening in both directions?

For finishes on both faces, yes: each face is its own line with its own deduction.

Can a deduction have a thickness?

Yes. Its volume is then negative too, which is how a shaft is taken out of a slab’s concrete.
