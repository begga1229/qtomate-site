Drawing layers

Named sets of lines in a drawing, such as walls, dimensions, text or one engineering system, that can be shown or hidden together.

CAD drawings are organised in layers. When a sheet is exported to PDF with layers kept, the PDF calls them optional content, and viewers can switch them on and off.

Hiding what you are not measuring makes a busy sheet readable and stops you from clicking the wrong line. Layer names often follow a convention, like A-WALL or a project prefix, which makes them easy to find.

Worked example

A services sheet exported with well over a hundred layers, a few dozen of them on the current page: hide everything except the cable routes and the devices, and only those lines can be clicked.

In QtoMate

Open Layers (K) to see the layers on the current page or in the whole file, search by name, and hide or show them all at once. Your choice is saved with the file.

Questions

Why does my PDF show no layers?

The layers were not kept when it was exported or printed. Ask for a PDF exported with layers if you need them.

Does hiding a layer change my measurements?

No. It only changes what is drawn and what the cursor can snap to.

Related terms

  • SnappingMaking the cursor land on an exact point of the drawing, such as a line end or a crossing, instead of wherever you clicked.
  • Count takeoffCounting items priced per piece: doors, windows, sockets, detectors, fittings.
  • Linear takeoffMeasuring items priced by length: walls, skirting, cable, pipe, kerbs, handrails.
